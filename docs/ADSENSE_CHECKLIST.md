# Google AdSense checklist

Approval is never guaranteed and Google changes its rules, so confirm each point on Google's AdSense help pages before applying. This list is what we know and plan.

## Needed before applying
- [ ] **Own domain name** (a custom domain; free subdomains are usually refused) and **HTTPS/SSL** working. Waits for hosting.
- [ ] **Real, original content.** Renuga's actual novels, not sample data. Enough chapters visible without login (reading is open, which helps). Remove all "(மாதிரி)" sample items.
- [ ] **No placeholder text anywhere.** Replace "[Short author introduction goes here]" and every [bracketed] text on the required pages.
- [ ] **Pages:** About Us, Contact Us (working email), Privacy Policy (mentions cookies and Google advertising), Terms, FAQ. Create with Tools > DM Required Pages, then fill and publish.
- [ ] **Easy navigation:** menu and footer links work, no broken links, no "under construction" pages.
- [ ] **Site works on phone and desktop**, loads fast, readable text.
- [ ] **Cookie consent banner** for visitors from the EEA/UK/Switzerland (Google needs a consent tool for them). Choose a Google-certified one when we add ads.
- [ ] Some history of regular publishing helps. The author's weekly chapters fit this.

## When approved
- [ ] Add the AdSense code and an `ads.txt` file at the site root.
- [ ] Place ads in reserved slots (class `rn-ad`) so layout does not jump. Never place ads over the reading controls, never in front of the chapter title, and never encourage clicks.
- [ ] Do not click our own ads. Google can ban the account.

## Build rules to protect approval
- Reading stays free without login.
- No copied content, no auto-generated filler.
- Login-only areas (comments, bookmarks) are not counted as the main content.
- Keep ads away from the reading controls and the first screen of a chapter.

## Open questions
- Which domain name?
- Who owns the AdSense account (Renuga or Kavi)? Payments go to the account owner.
- Is a cookie consent tool needed for her audience? Likely yes if any visitors come from Europe.
